How it works

One pipeline, running every frame of your session.

01

Read shared memory

PitWatch reads live telemetry directly from ACC or LMU's shared memory, the same data the sim itself uses.

02

Ask, wheel-bound

Bind any free button on your wheel in PitWatch. Hold it and ask your question out loud.

via Windows dictation for now, native voice input is coming soon
03

Gemini reasons about it

Your question and the live race state are interpreted by the Gemini API, which decides what's worth saying and when.

04

Speak on your radio

The response is spoken back over your comms, triggered hands-free from the same wheel button.

HOW IT WORKS
Three systems, one radio channel.

Telemetry in, reasoning in the middle, a voice back out — the same loop a real race engineer runs, just running constantly and never getting tired.

Telemetry, straight from the sim

PitWatch reads ACC and Le Mans Ultimate's shared memory directly — position, gaps, damage, fuel, tyre wear — with nothing installed on the sim side and no ports exposed.

→ feeds the engineer

An engineer that actually reasons

Every frame runs through the Gemini API. PitWatch isn't replaying canned lines — it's weighing what just happened against what mattered, lap by lap.

→ decides what to say

Ask it, out loud

Click into PitWatch's input field, use Windows dictation to ask your question, and get a spoken answer back. Native voice input is coming soon, so you won't need Windows dictation at all.

→ speaks on the radio
FEATURES
Everything a good engineer would tell you.

PitWatch doesn't wait to be asked for the things that matter most — it calls them the moment they happen.

Overtake commentary

Calls the pass as it happens — who, where, and how big the gap was, without you having to check a leaderboard.

Crash & contact detection

Flags contact the instant it happens, so you know if it's worth a look before the next braking zone.

Damage callouts

Tells you what broke and whether it's safe to keep pushing — not just that something happened.

Race finish messages

A proper radio sign-off when you take the flag — your finishing position and the shape of the race that got you there.

Push-to-talk Q&A

Ask "what's my gap to P3" or "how much fuel have I got" by selecting PitWatch's input field and dictating your question, then hear the answer straight away.

Built for ACC & LMU

Native shared-memory support for Assetto Corsa Competizione and Le Mans Ultimate today, with more sims planned.

FAQ
Before you ask the radio.
What do I need to run it?
ACC or Le Mans Ultimate on PC. Right now, you click into PitWatch's input field and use Windows dictation to ask your question. We're patching that soon so PitWatch handles voice input natively, no Windows dictation required.
Does this replace my spotter overlay?
No — it replaces the need to look at one. PitWatch is built to be heard, not read, so your eyes stay on the track.
Where does my telemetry go?
Telemetry is read locally from the sim's shared memory. Only the race context PitWatch needs to answer or call something is sent out for reasoning.
Which sims are supported?
Assetto Corsa Competizione is fully supported today, with Le Mans Ultimate in active testing. More sims are planned after launch.
